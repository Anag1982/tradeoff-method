# Artefact sheets

`artefacts.pdf` is seven A4 pages. The first five are the artefacts every design in the book produces, in the order the clock produces them: the objective sheet (name the objective), the envelope (bound the problem), the tradeoff table with the budget triangle (choose under the constraint), the decision line with space for the minute-fifteen diagram, and the loop sheet (close the loop). Pages six and seven are the two sheets Chapter 15's mock protocol uses: the timing sheet the partner fills in, and the self-scoring sheet both fill in against Chapter 16's rubric.

Print one set per mock. The source is `artefacts.tex`; `pdflatex artefacts.tex` rebuilds it.
