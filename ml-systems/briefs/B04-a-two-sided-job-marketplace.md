# B.4 A two-sided job marketplace

## Candidate's half

Read only this section before starting the clock.

A job board wants to recommend candidates to employers and jobs to candidates, and the business is paid when a hire happens.

---

## Interviewer's half

Give the numbers only if asked. Deliver the three pushes in the questions block whatever the candidate has said, and the twist at minute thirty. Score against the rubric notes below and the sheets in `../rubrics/`; the notes name the row that decides the brief, never the design.

### Numbers, if asked

Five million active candidates; half a million open jobs; a hire is reported for a tenth of jobs, weeks after the application; applications are the plentiful signal; employers respond to a fifth of applications; a candidate shown to too many employers is over-applied and stops responding; a job shown to too many candidates drowns the employer.

### Pushes

The recommender sends every candidate to the same fifty jobs; what happened? An employer complains that the candidates shown are all from one university. The hire label arrives six weeks late and is missing for jobs filled off the platform.

### Twist, at minute thirty

Regulation requires that recommendations not disadvantage candidates by age, and age is inferrable from the graduation year on every profile.
