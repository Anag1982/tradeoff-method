# Staff rubric

The rows of the senior rubric with the anchors moved. A staff prompt is a situation, not a system, and half the score is earned before anything is drawn. Score each row and note the minute.

| Row | Below the bar | At the bar | Above the bar | Minute |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Frame** | Accepts the proposed solution as the problem ("get off the database", "launch in Asia") and starts designing it. | Asks what the request is solving; gets an objective. | Turns the solution into a problem with a number and a date; finds the constraint that is not stated (residency, a ceiling, an ownership boundary) by asking; names the hard part. | |
| **Budget** | Budgets requests when the brief is about time, risk or money. | Budgets the right quantity for the brief. | Budgets time to the wall, the capacity every step must not exceed, and the cost of running two of something; states the deadline the first change must land inside. | |
| **Axes** | Enumerates designs. | Names the axis the situation turns on. | Names the axis and the structural choice that avoids the hard problem rather than solving it (one writer per entity; capture from the log; a threshold between shared and dedicated). | |
| **Commit** | Proposes the largest design. | Chooses, with a reason. | Chooses the cheapest design that meets the objective and says so even when it is unglamorous; names at least one rejected alternative with a reason from the brief; sequences the work by reversibility. | |
| **Deepen** | Goes broad across five things. | Works one mechanism properly. | Works the one mechanism the whole plan rests on (the reverse capture, the failover decision) and states the invariant it protects. | |
| **Stress** | Defends. | Runs the four attacks. | Runs the four attacks and the fifth: names the irreversible step, places it last, and says what evidence must exist before it is taken; says what they will not do and why. | |

**The three verbs** the sheet rewards: decide rather than enumerate; cut complexity rather than display it; go deep on one thing rather than broad across five.

**Readiness signal:** the brief sheet complete by minute eight with an objective that has a date; one rejected alternative named with a reason; the irreversible step named unprompted.
