# Senior rubric

Score each row below, at, or above the bar. Note the minute at which the row's artefact appeared.

| Row | Below the bar | At the bar | Above the bar | Minute |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Frame** (brief sheet) | Takes the prompt as given; draws within three minutes; asks no question that changes the design. | States an objective and one non-negotiable; scopes something out; proposes a number. | Names the hard part before drawing; proposes all the numbers and is corrected on few; the brief sheet is complete by minute eight. | |
| **Budget** (envelope) | No arithmetic, or arithmetic without a conclusion. | Rate and storage computed to an order of magnitude; a latency budget. | Finds the line that binds and says so; cost per thousand; fan-out and the tail; the numbers reappear on the diagram. | |
| **Axes** (tradeoff table) | Lists technologies rather than tradeoffs; "it depends" with no dependence named. | Names two or three tradeoffs with both ends. | Three to five rows, each with the direction the brief points and the price; identifies the axis the brief turns on. | |
| **Commit** (decision lines) | Offers options and waits to be told; or commits with no reason. | Chooses, with a reason from the brief. | Decision lines with the alternative and the accepted price; changes a decision when a number changes, and says why. | |
| **Deepen** (the hard part) | Adds components; describes a technology's features. | Works one mechanism to the point where its failure mode is visible. | The mechanism, its numbers and the failure it prevents, with the sensitivity named unprompted. | |
| **Stress** (four attacks) | Waits for the interviewer to attack; defends rather than prices. | Answers the pushes; concedes a weakness. | Attacks the design first; each attack ends in a hold, an addition or a re-frame; says who is paged and what they do first. | |

**Not scored:** breadth of technologies named; completeness of the diagram on its own.

**Readiness signal:** brief sheet by 8, envelope by 13, first decision line by 16, stress started by 28, without looking at the clock.
